This was an app that was more of an introduction to C++, it was to teach us how to use inputs and manipulate data in the correct ways as well as formatting stuff to be shown back to the user. It’s just a manually changed clock running in the console where you can add seconds, minutes, and hours, or you can exit based on the user input.
